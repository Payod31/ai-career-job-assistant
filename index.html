What this tool does:

Two clearly-labelled modes, no surprises:

  • Personalized target roles: above, CareerAI suggests roles from the skills & experience actually on your resume, and explains why each fits. Add the roles you want to search (or a custom title), then use the filters and "Get Job-Board Search Links".
  • Job-board search: pre-filled external links into LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri and Monster built from your chosen target roles + filters. These are links you open, not listings fetched by this app.
  • Live listings — not available yet: an explicit placeholder. No vacancies, salaries or companies are invented or implied. A real listing source would need an authorized job API; none is wired in yet.
